What is a 40g energy gel and how does it work?

Our 40g Energy Gel is designed to provide fast, efficient fuel during endurance activities such as running, cycling, triathlon, and long training sessions. It uses a dual-carbohydrate system that combines multiple carbohydrate sources to help deliver energy quickly while supporting sustained performance. The gel is rapidly absorbed, easy to consume on the move, and helps replenish the energy your muscles use during prolonged exercise.

When should I take the gel during training or racing?

For best results, consume one gel 5–15 minutes before activity or during exercise when your energy demands increase. During longer sessions, athletes commonly take a gel every 30–45 minutes depending on intensity, duration, and individual carbohydrate needs. Practicing your fueling strategy during training is recommended before race day.

Do I need to take it with water?

Our dual-carb formulation is designed for easy consumption during exercise. While the gel can be taken on its own, consuming water alongside any energy gel can improve comfort and hydration, especially during long events or in hot conditions. Always follow the hydration strategy that works best for your sport and conditions.

How many gels can I consume per hour?

The ideal amount depends on your body size, exercise intensity, and duration. Because our gel uses a dual-carbohydrate system, it is designed to support higher carbohydrate intake during endurance exercise compared to single-source carbohydrate products. Many endurance athletes aim for a consistent carbohydrate intake throughout their session, using multiple gels over longer durations as part of their fueling plan.

Will it cause stomach discomfort or energy crashes?

Our gel is formulated with a dual-carb blend to support efficient carbohydrate delivery and absorption during exercise. By utilizing multiple carbohydrate pathways, the formulation is designed to help provide a steadier supply of energy while improving gastrointestinal tolerance during prolonged efforts. As with any sports nutrition product, individual tolerance can vary, so we recommend testing your fueling strategy during training before competition.

What makes the dual-carb formulation better?

Traditional gels often rely on a single carbohydrate source. Our dual-carb approach combines complementary carbohydrate sources that utilize different transport mechanisms in the body. This helps support efficient carbohydrate delivery during endurance exercise, making it easier for athletes to fuel consistently over long distances while maintaining performance when it matters most.
